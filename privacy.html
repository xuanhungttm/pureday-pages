PureDay Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

PureDay is private by design — for this kind of journey, privacy is the product. Everything you log — check-ins, urge sessions, slip debriefs, your reasons and your letter to yourself — is stored only on your iPhone. There is no account, no community, no PureDay server, and we cannot see your data.

What is collected

  • Your logs and settings: stored on-device only (SwiftData). Deleted permanently when you use "Delete all my data" in Settings or delete the app.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are processed by Apple and managed with RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives purchase receipts and an anonymous app user ID to unlock Pro. No purchase data is linked to your identity by us.
  • Notifications: reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Their text is neutral and never names your habit.
  • Face ID: if you enable the discreet lock, authentication happens entirely through iOS. We never receive or store biometric data.

What is NOT collected

No analytics SDKs, no advertising trackers, no location, no contacts, no health data, and your logs are never uploaded, shared or sold.

Wellness note

PureDay is a self-tracking and wellness tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition or addiction. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (US Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, free, 24/7).

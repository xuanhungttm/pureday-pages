FAQ

How does the 90-day reboot work? Your program runs in four stages — Reset (days 1–7), Rewire (8–30), Rebuild (31–60) and Sustain (61–90) — with one short mission and a 2-minute check-in each day.

What happens when an urge hits? Tap PANIC. Urge Wave runs a 10-minute session: urges typically peak around minute 4 and then pass. You pick one physical action, breathe with the pacer, and your own letter to yourself appears mid-session.

I slipped. Did I lose everything? No. A slip is data, not defeat. The Slip Debrief asks four quick questions, keeps all your past clean days, and only the current streak restarts — so your patterns get clearer, not erased.

What is Danger Window? After a few logged sessions, PureDay finds the hour your urges hit hardest and (with Pro) reminds you shortly before it — with neutral wording that never names your habit.

Is my data really private? Yes. No account, no cloud, no community. With Pro you can also lock the app with Face ID.

Restore a purchase: Settings → Restore purchases.

Need more support? In the US, call or text 988 any time. You can also find peer support at SMART Recovery (smartrecovery.org) and Sex Addicts Anonymous (saa-recovery.org). PureDay is a self-tracking tool, not a medical device.
